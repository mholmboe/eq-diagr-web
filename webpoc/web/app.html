        Eq-Diagr

        Chemical equilibrium diagrams with the programs of Ignasi Puigdomènech (DataBase, Spana, SED, Predom, HaltaFall and their tools), compiled from Java to JavaScript and run in this browser. The calculations are the same as in the desktop programs, and your files and databases stay on this computer.

        Normal mode shows the options needed for most diagrams. Advanced mode adds the calculation options, the table and debugging output, merging chemical systems, and the database maintenance tools, as the advanced preference of the desktop programs does.

        Visits to this site are counted with GoatCounter (goatcounter.com): when the app starts, the address of the page, the page you came from and the width of the screen are reported, without cookies. Your files, databases and diagrams are not sent.
