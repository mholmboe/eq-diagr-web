Chemical Equilibrium Diagrams / Tutorials

Make your first diagram

This diagram shows the hydrolysis of iron(II) as a function of pH. It takes four clicks in the Chemical system tab and one in the diagram tab.

  1. In the Chemical system tab, click Fe in the periodic table. The components of iron are listed under Available for Fe. Click Fe 2+. The selected components are now H+ (selected from the start) and Fe2+.
  2. Click Search reactions. The reactions found in the database are listed with their log K values and references.
  3. Click Create a diagram. The data file is saved in this browser (as H+_Fe2+.dat), and a new tab opens with the diagram settings. Spana has already chosen a logarithmic diagram with pH on the X-axis and a total concentration of 10 µM for iron(II).
  4. Click Plot the diagram (or press Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter on a Mac). SED calculates the equilibrium composition at 51 pH values and draws the diagram.

The logarithmic diagram shows the distribution of the hydrolysis products of Fe(II) as a function of pH. Fe(OH)2 precipitates above pH ≈ 9. The picture above was made with the desktop programs and the eq-diagr database; with the KTH course database, the default, the solid is called Fe(OH)2(c) and the curves are nearly the same.

From here you can change the concentrations (in the Concentrations section), choose another diagram type, or export the diagram with the buttons above it (SVG, PNG, PDF). See making a diagram.

Two things to try with this diagram:

  • Point at the diagram. The pH and the logarithm of the concentration at the pointer are shown under the diagram, together with the species at that pH, for example where the curves of Fe2+ and FeOH+ cross (see reading values from the diagram).
  • Tick Live beside Plot the diagram, and drag the slider that appears under the total concentration of Fe2+. The diagram follows the slider, and the pH at which Fe(OH)2 starts to precipitate moves with the concentration (see live mode).

The data file stays in this browser and is listed under Data files on the left. Click it there to open it again later. See files in the browser.
