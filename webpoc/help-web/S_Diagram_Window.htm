SPANA

The diagram

When SED or PREDOM has finished, the diagram is drawn on the right of the diagram tab (below the settings on a narrow screen). It is drawn from the plot file that the program wrote, the same file the desktop Spana displays, and it is scaled to the space available. The labels are drawn as text, with charges as superscripts and indices as subscripts as in the desktop Spana; see the diagram style.

Each diagram tab shows the diagram of its own data file, so several diagrams can be compared by switching between tabs. When a setting is changed, the diagram is dimmed and marked "Settings changed – Plot the diagram" until it is made again; the diagram style is the only setting that changes the diagram at once. In live mode the diagram is instead made again after each change.

The diagram style

Style… above the diagram (or View > Diagram style…) sets how all diagrams are drawn. The style is remembered in this browser and used by every diagram tab.

Labelssans-serif (Helvetica, Arial; the default), serif (Times), monospaced (Courier), or the plotter lines of the plot file, as the desktop programs draw them without a font.
Text size80 to 150 % of the size in the plot file. To give the diagram larger texts and room for them, use Text height in the Options (advanced mode) instead.
Curve coloursthe colours of the desktop programs, a palette that can be told apart with colour-vision deficiencies (Okabe and Ito), black and white, white on black, or your own colours: with my own colours, a colour can be chosen for each of the eleven pens of the plot files (pen 1 draws the axes and the texts).
Texts above the diagramthe programs write the fixed concentrations, the temperature and the ionic strength above the diagram. Untick the ones you do not want in the drawing and in the exported files.
Width75 to 150 % of the width the programs give the diagram. The lettering keeps its shape; only the positions along the X-axis change.
Linesthe curves: thin, normal or thick.
Tick marksinside the diagram (as the programs draw them), outside, or crossing the axis.
Minor ticksnone, or 2 or 5 for each step between the numbers.
Grid linesnone, at the numbers, or at all ticks.
Frameas the programs draw it (SED: the two axes; PREDOM: a box), the axes only, or a box, with the ticks repeated on the top and right sides.
Axis linesthin, normal or thick.

The axis options need labels drawn with a font. With the plotter lines, the axes are those of the programs.

Comparing diagrams: what if…

Keep above the diagram (or Run > Keep the diagram for comparison) keeps the diagram shown. Change a setting, for example a total concentration, the ionic strength or a log K, or untick a reaction, and make the diagram again: the kept diagram stays in the drawing with broken lines, in lighter colours and without its labels, under the new one. Up to six diagrams can be kept, each with its own line pattern, and live mode shows the new diagram move over the kept ones while you drag a slider.

A legend under the diagram says what differs: the present diagram first, then each kept one, for example “Fe 2+ total 1e-5” against “Fe 2+ total 0.001”. The same names are listed under the drawing, where a click on a name lets you type one of your own and × removes that diagram. The kept diagrams and the legend are part of the SVG, PNG and PDF files, of the copied image and of the print. The .csv file of a SED diagram then has the table of the diagram shown followed by the tables of the kept diagrams, each under a line with its name. The .dat file is always the data file as it is now; the data files of the kept diagrams are not exported.

A kept diagram is drawn in the axes of the present one, also when the ranges of the axes have changed. A diagram of another kind (other quantities on the axes) cannot be drawn in it and is only named in the legend. The kept diagrams belong to the tab and are gone when it is closed.

The step between the numbers of an axis

The programs choose the numbers on the axes. In advanced mode, the Options of a diagram have Tick step, X-axis and Tick step, Y-axis: give a step in the units of the axis (for example 1 for every pH unit, or 0.1 for Eh in volts), or leave it empty for the program's choice. The step changes the drawing at once, without a new calculation. It is kept in this browser for the data file, not in the data file itself, and it is not used in normal mode.

The range of an axis is part of the calculation: change the range of the component in the X-axis (under Concentrations) or of the Y-axis row of the same table, and make the diagram again.

PNG image, in the same dialog, sets the size of the PNG file and of the image copied to the clipboard: 800 to 4800 pixels on the longest side (1600 unless changed).

The style applies to the screen and to the SVG, PNG and PDF files (the PDF with the standard PDF fonts, which are not embedded). EPS files (advanced mode) and plot files keep the plotter lettering of the programs, whatever the style; EPS is in black and white when the style is black and white.

Below the diagram:

  • Program messages: the output of SED or PREDOM, with warnings and the points where the calculation failed (see the programs fail? and strange results?);
  • Data file (.dat) (advanced mode): the data file as it was used;
  • Table (.csv, SED) (advanced mode): the values of the curves as a table, see table output.

Reading values from the diagram

Point at the diagram with the mouse, or touch it, to read the coordinates of the point. They are shown under the diagram in the units of the axes, for example pH and ESHE in volts, and a cross-hair marks the point. The cross-hair is drawn on the screen only, and is not in the exported or printed diagram.

For the SED diagrams that show fractions, solubilities, logarithmic concentrations or logarithmic activities, a second line lists the species at the X-value of the pointer, with the largest value first (at most six, and only those inside the range of the Y-axis). The values are interpolated between the points of the table of results, so with few calculation steps they are approximate near a sharp bend of a curve, for instance where a solid starts to form. The line is not shown when the table is not in the units of the axis (for example total concentrations drawn in mM), or when the table format was changed in the Options.

For exact values, download the table of results (.csv above the diagram, SED diagrams) and open it in a spreadsheet.
